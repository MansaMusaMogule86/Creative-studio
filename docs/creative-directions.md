# Mansas Moguls: three creative directions

All three stay inside the brief: predominantly light, built on the approved palette (Pearl `#F8F7FF`, White, Electric lavender `#B9A5FF`, Digital aqua `#8EE7E8`, Soft peach `#FFC6AC`, Deep ink `#25243C`), with no black-and-gold, no cyberpunk darkness and no generic SaaS gradients.

---

## A. Liquid Pearl ★ recommended, prototyped

| | |
|---|---|
| **Visual concept** | A studio product shot of the future. One sculptural liquid-glass object floats on warm pearl, lit by soft light cards in lavender, aqua and peach. Iridescence comes from the material, not from painted gradients. The rest of the site is calm editorial paper, so the sculpture is the only loud thing. |
| **Colour system** | 90% pearl and white. Lavender, aqua and peach appear as *light* (reflections, glows, artwork) rather than as fills. Deep ink carries all type and primary buttons. Each division takes one accent trio, used only inside its world. |
| **Typography** | Instrument Serif (high-contrast editorial display, upright caps and an italic for the emotional half of a line) with Outfit (geometric sans for UI and body). |
| **Hero concept** | "WE ARCHITECT" in ink capitals, with "*what's next.*" in an iridescent italic. Beside it, a real-time WebGL ribbon of iridescent glass traces the monogram's twin arches, twisting slowly and leaning toward the cursor. |
| **Logo direction** | **Twin Arch**: two interlocking arches form the M. Architecture (the arch), connection (the shared pillar) and progress (the second arch rising past the first). It works as one stroke weight in holo, ink, white and favicon, and the hero sculpture is its 3D form. |
| **Motion behaviour** | Slow, weighted, physical: a 2-second entrance, ease-out curves and ambient drift. Scroll only *reveals*; it never pins or hijacks. Reduced motion renders one still frame. |
| **Mobile** | The sculpture moves above the headline, at a lower vertex count and a pixel ratio capped at 1.5. It drifts on its own, with no cursor needed. Division hovers become a tap accordion and service tabs become a swipeable chip row. |

## B. Spatial Atrium

| | |
|---|---|
| **Visual concept** | Spatial-computing architecture: frosted planes floating in a bright atrium, with thin grid lines, depth layers and soft shadows. It feels like walking through a glass building. |
| **Colour system** | White dominant, with an aqua-tinted glass. Lavender for depth, peach reserved for calls to action. |
| **Typography** | A tight neo-grotesk display (Swiss feel) with a monospaced caption face for labels. |
| **Hero concept** | The M is extruded as two folded architectural planes. The camera dollies slowly through them as the page loads. |
| **Logo direction** | A folded-plane M, like origami or a canopy roof, drawn as two parallelograms meeting at a crease. |
| **Motion behaviour** | Parallax depth, plane separation on hover and precise linear easing. |
| **Mobile** | The planes flatten into stacked cards, and the depth comes from shadows only. |
| **Risk** | It leans toward "premium SaaS". It is elegant, but less ownable. |

## C. Living Signal

| | |
|---|---|
| **Visual concept** | Generative digital organisms: particle fields that gather into forms and dissolve, like the company's intelligence made visible. |
| **Colour system** | Pearl ground. Particles sweep through a lavender to aqua to peach spectrum. |
| **Typography** | A variable sans whose weight animates with the particles, with a mono for data-like labels. |
| **Hero concept** | Thousands of particles assemble into the M, then scatter toward the cursor and regroup. |
| **Logo direction** | A dotted M: a field of points with a clear negative-space spine. |
| **Motion behaviour** | Constant, reactive and swarm-like. It is the most "alive", and the hardest to keep calm. |
| **Mobile** | Fewer particles, assembled once, then breathing gently. |
| **Risk** | It is easy to drift into "AI startup" clichés, the busiest of the three, and the heaviest on GPU and battery. |

---

## Why Liquid Pearl

1. **It matches the references.** Both supplied images are iridescent liquid-glass sculptures on soft light grounds. A is that language made interactive.
2. **It is ownable.** The sculpture *is* the logo in 3D, so the hero, the favicon and the mark tell one story. B and C use more common visual vocabularies.
3. **It has range.** A calm editorial base lets each division bring its own artwork and motion without the site feeling noisy. C is too loud a base for that, and B too cold.
4. **It performs.** One WebGL scene, loaded after first paint and paused when off-screen. Everything else is CSS and SVG.

Ideas worth borrowing later: B's frosted depth planes (already echoed in the Digital artwork) and C's particle organism (already used for the AI division).
